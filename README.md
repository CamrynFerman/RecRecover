## Rec Room for Meta Quest: 2023 build research

**Status:** No 2023 Quest APK/OBB is included. No source reviewed here establishes permission to redistribute Rec Room's proprietary client.

### What could be verified

- Rec Room's official ship note for **November 28, 2023**, titled “Rec Room PATCH - the ‘Coming to a Beta Near You’ edition,” mentions a fix for avatar and outfit thumbnails **on Quest**. It confirms a Quest-related update on that date, but does not publish a Quest app version/build number, package, or checksum: [official ship note](https://recroom.com/ship-notes/2023/11/28/rec-room-patch-the-coming-to-a-beta-near-you-edition).
- A third-party archive lists an **“RR Early November 2023 Build Switch”** and describes it as an NSP for a modded Switch. That is not a Meta Quest build and is not a legitimate Quest acquisition source: [archive release listing](https://github.com/RecCoach/RecRoomFiles/releases/tag/Switch-Early-Nov-2023).
- RecRoomArchive describes Radium as a preservation project based on a 2023 release, but says it is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rec Room Inc. or its IP owner**. That does not provide redistribution authorization: [RecRoomArchive](https://www.recroomarchive.org/).

### Licensing and acquisition

Rec Room's [Terms of Service](https://recroom.com/terms-of-service) restrict copying and distributing software obtained from the game/services. No license or written permission authorizing redistribution of a 2023 Quest client was found during this research. A public download or an archival/preservation purpose alone is not evidence of such permission.

For an authorized copy, use only a build Rec Room/the rights holder or Meta makes available through an official channel, or obtain **written permission and an authorized source copy directly from the rights holder** before considering any repository distribution. The official [Rec Room download page](https://rec.net/download) is the starting point for currently offered downloads; it does not establish availability of a historical 2023 version. Do not use third-party APK/OBB mirrors as a substitute for authorization.

If the rights holder supplies an authorized 2023 Quest build, record its exact app version/build, Quest model compatibility, source and acquisition date, package filenames, and SHA-256 hashes here. Add the binary only if the permission explicitly covers redistribution in this public repository.
